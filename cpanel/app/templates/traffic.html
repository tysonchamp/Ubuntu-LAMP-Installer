How it works

Lite-cPanel uses GoAccess to parse Nginx and Apache access logs in real-time. Each domain configured as a proxy has its own dedicated log file located in /var/log/nginx/ or /var/log/apache2/.

"Full Reports" are generated dynamically and provide deep insights into browsers, OS, status codes, and geographical data of your visitors.
